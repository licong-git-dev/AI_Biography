# Sample C 模型投喂 Prompt 拆分包

## 全局风格前缀

```text
warm romantic realism, modern Chinese urban life, gentle natural light, creamy highlights, cinematic softness, healing emotional tone, photorealistic, subtle intimacy, contemporary China
```

## 全局负面约束

```text
avoid idol-drama exaggeration, avoid anime style, avoid glossy ad look, avoid fantasy lighting, avoid distorted hands, avoid plastic skin, avoid text in image, avoid logo, avoid watermark
```

---

## C-01 Image Prompt：第一眼相遇

```text
A young Chinese man looking up through a lively social gathering and noticing a young woman for the first time, subtle pause in time, warm natural light, emotionally charged but understated, modern Chinese urban realism, soft cinematic texture.
```

## C-01 Video Prompt

```text
A slight pause as the young man raises his eyes through the crowd and notices her, subtle breathing and eye focus shift, gentle warm light, restrained emotional pull, no exaggerated romance.
```

---

## C-02 Image Prompt：回头一笑

```text
Close-up of a young Chinese woman turning back with a warm, gentle smile, clean modern styling, soft flattering daylight, healing and romantic atmosphere, cinematic shallow depth of field, photorealistic, elegant emotional realism.
```

## C-02 Video Prompt

```text
The young woman turns back slowly and smiles with natural warmth, hair moving slightly, clean soft light, no exaggerated expression, intimate and believable.
```

---

## C-03 Image Prompt：毛坯房第一次相处

```text
A modest unfinished apartment interior in Wuhan, a young Chinese man awkwardly preparing simple food like sausage and soup dumplings for a young woman visiting for the first time, humble yet sincere atmosphere, warm domestic light, modern Chinese realism.
```

## C-04 Image Prompt：骑车过江与小吃蒙太奇

```text
A romantic urban montage in Wuhan: two young people riding shared bikes, crossing the river by ferry, eating local street snacks together, natural laughter, relaxed companionship, warm evening light, realistic Chinese city life, cinematic travel-date feeling.
```

## C-05 Image Prompt：九锅一堂长谈

```text
Intimate medium shot in a cozy Chinese restaurant at night, two young people talking deeply about family and childhood, guarded expressions slowly turning into trust, warm table light, realistic conversation energy, emotionally healing, cinematic realism.
```

## C-05 Video Prompt

```text
A quiet emotionally intimate restaurant conversation, both speaking softly, trust gradually building in their expressions, warm table light, no dramatic gestures, believable modern Chinese realism.
```

---

## C-06 Image Prompt：蓝毛衣做饭

```text
A young Chinese woman in a blue sweater crouches in a modest unfinished apartment, cooking a simple homemade potato chicken dish on a small stove, humble domestic tenderness, warm kitchen light, realistic detail, healing emotional tone.
```

## C-07 Image Prompt：牵手与表白蒙太奇

```text
A romantic montage of modern Chinese love: holding hands on a bridge, strawberry picking, a carefully prepared confession, warm daily life together, mutual healing, understated but sincere affection, cinematic romantic realism.
```

## C-08 Image Prompt：对视定格

```text
An emotional medium close-up of a young Chinese couple looking at each other quietly, soft background blur, gentle warm light, a feeling of safety and belonging, elegant stillness, cinematic romance, photorealistic.
```

## C-08 Video Prompt

```text
A still intimate moment of two people looking at each other quietly, very slight breathing and eye movement, warm soft light, emotional calm and safety, ideal for final freeze-frame or end shot.
```
