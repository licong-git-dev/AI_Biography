# Sample C Prompt Pack

## 样片
- 名称：遇见她以后
- 风格统一词：warm romantic realism, modern Chinese urban life, gentle natural light, creamy highlights, cinematic softness, healing emotional tone

## Prompt C-01 第一眼相遇
**中文描述**：青年李聪在人群里抬头，看见她。

**Video Prompt**
```text
Cinematic medium close-up in a lively social gathering, a young Chinese man looks up through the crowd and notices a young woman for the first time, subtle pause in time, warm natural light, emotionally charged but understated, modern Chinese urban realism, soft cinematic texture.
```

## Prompt C-02 回头一笑
**中文描述**：张珂回头一笑，光线温柔。

**Video Prompt**
```text
Close-up of a young Chinese woman turning back with a warm, gentle smile, clean modern styling, soft flattering daylight, healing and romantic atmosphere, cinematic shallow depth of field, creamy highlights, photorealistic, elegant emotional realism.
```

## Prompt C-03 毛坯房第一次相处
**中文描述**：毛坯房里蒸腊肠、汤包，第一次相处。

**Video Prompt**
```text
A modest unfinished apartment interior in Wuhan, a young man awkwardly preparing simple food like sausage and soup dumplings for a young woman visiting for the first time, humble yet sincere atmosphere, modern Chinese urban realism, warm domestic light, cinematic storytelling.
```

## Prompt C-04 骑车过江与小吃
**中文描述**：共享单车、轮渡、粮道街小吃。

**Video Prompt**
```text
A romantic urban montage in Wuhan: two young people riding shared bikes, crossing the river by ferry, eating local street snacks together, natural laughter, relaxed companionship, warm evening light, realistic Chinese city life, cinematic travel-date feeling.
```

## Prompt C-05 九锅一堂长谈
**中文描述**：在餐馆里聊起彼此家庭。

**Video Prompt**
```text
Intimate medium shot in a cozy Chinese restaurant at night, two young people talking deeply about family and childhood, guarded expressions slowly turning into trust, warm table light, realistic conversation energy, emotionally healing, cinematic realism.
```

## Prompt C-06 毛坯房做饭
**中文描述**：蓝毛衣、毛坯房、她蹲着做土豆烧鸡。

**Video Prompt**
```text
A young Chinese woman in a blue sweater crouches in a modest unfinished apartment, cooking a simple homemade potato chicken dish on a small stove, humble domestic tenderness, warm kitchen light, realistic detail, healing emotional tone, cinematic close observation.
```

## Prompt C-07 牵手与表白蒙太奇
**中文描述**：过桥牵手、摘草莓、表白、在一起后的日常。

**Image Prompt**
```text
A romantic montage of modern Chinese love: holding hands on a bridge, strawberry picking, a carefully prepared confession, warm daily life together, mutual healing, understated but sincere affection, cinematic romantic realism, soft warm palette.
```

## Prompt C-08 对视定格
**中文描述**：两人对视，适合作封面和结尾定格。

**Video Prompt**
```text
An emotional medium close-up of a young Chinese couple looking at each other quietly, soft background blur, gentle warm light, a feeling of safety and belonging, elegant stillness, cinematic romance, photorealistic, ideal for final freeze-frame cover shot.
```
