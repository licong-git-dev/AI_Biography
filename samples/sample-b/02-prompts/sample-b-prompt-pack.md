# Sample B Prompt Pack

## 样片
- 名称：那个不说话的男孩
- 风格统一词：Chinese youth realism, restrained emotion, grey-blue school tones, warm-and-cold contrast, cinematic 35mm texture, early-2000s to early-2010s China

## Prompt B-01 校门口
**中文描述**：少年李聪独自走进校园。

**Video Prompt**
```text
Cinematic tracking shot of a Chinese teenage boy walking alone into a school gate with a backpack, morning light, ordinary Chinese campus atmosphere, restrained and lonely mood, slight grey-blue school color palette, vintage 35mm texture, realistic youth drama.
```

## Prompt B-02 教室打量
**中文描述**：老师点名，同学看向他。

**Video Prompt**
```text
Medium shot in a Chinese classroom, the teacher calls a student's name and the whole class turns to look at a teenage boy, subtle tension, realistic school environment, quiet social pressure, cinematic composition, restrained emotional realism, soft film grain.
```

## Prompt B-03 角落特写
**中文描述**：教室角落里紧绷的眼神。

**Video Prompt**
```text
Emotional close-up of a Chinese teenage boy sitting in the corner of a classroom, tense jaw, guarded eyes, silent pressure, shallow depth of field, grey-blue tones, cinematic 35mm film look, subtle breathing motion, realistic and intimate.
```

## Prompt B-04 旧摩托接人
**中文描述**：妈妈骑旧摩托来接，周围是小汽车。

**Video Prompt**
```text
After-school scene outside a Chinese school, a mother arrives on an old motorcycle while other families pick children up in cars, a teenage boy feels shame and conflict, realistic city-town setting, emotional contrast, cinematic natural light, photorealistic.
```

## Prompt B-05 夜里盯着天花板
**中文描述**：房间夜晚，不说话地盯着天花板。

**Video Prompt**
```text
Night interior, a teenage boy lying awake staring at the ceiling in silence, city light leaking through the window, lonely and heavy emotional atmosphere, restrained performance, cool-and-warm contrast, cinematic close framing, vintage film texture.
```

## Prompt B-06 叛逆蒙太奇
**中文描述**：跆拳道、滑板、网吧、耳机里的音乐。

**Image Prompt**
```text
A montage of a Chinese boy's restless youth: taekwondo training in a small dojo, skateboarding wildly after school, smoky internet cafe gaming, earphones with Jay Chou songs, rebellious energy, early-2000s Chinese youth life, grey-blue and warm tungsten contrast, cinematic realism.
```

## Prompt B-07 冲突后的沉默
**中文描述**：与妈妈冲突后把眼泪憋回去。

**Video Prompt**
```text
Cinematic emotional medium close-up of a teenage boy after an argument with his mother, trying not to cry, lips pressed tight, eyes wet but holding back tears, domestic interior, heavy silence, realistic family drama, low-key lighting, vintage film grain.
```

## Prompt B-08 路灯背影
**中文描述**：放学后独自走在路灯下。

**Video Prompt**
```text
Long shot of a teenage boy walking home alone under streetlights after school, long shadow on the ground, quiet road, slightly cold night air, introspective and lonely, cinematic follow shot, Chinese youth realism, restrained emotion, soft film texture.
```
