# Sample B 模型投喂 Prompt 拆分包

## 全局风格前缀

```text
Chinese youth realism, restrained emotion, grey-blue school tones, warm-and-cold contrast, cinematic 35mm texture, early-2000s to early-2010s China, photorealistic, soft grain, realistic school and home environment
```

## 全局负面约束

```text
avoid anime style, avoid cyberpunk neon, avoid glossy idol drama look, avoid exaggerated crying face, avoid distorted hands, avoid overly dramatic lighting, avoid text in image, avoid logo, avoid watermark
```

---

## B-01 Image Prompt：校门口

```text
Chinese youth realism, restrained emotion, cinematic 35mm texture. A Chinese teenage boy walking alone into a school gate with a backpack, ordinary campus morning, subtle loneliness, realistic early-2000s to early-2010s China, soft grey-blue school tones, photorealistic.
```

## B-01 Video Prompt

```text
A gentle follow shot of a teenage boy walking alone into the school gate, slight hesitation in body language, calm school atmosphere, restrained and lonely mood, no exaggerated motion.
```

---

## B-02 Image Prompt：教室打量

```text
Inside a Chinese classroom, a teacher is calling roll while classmates turn to look at a teenage boy, quiet social pressure, ordinary desks and blackboard, realistic school setting, restrained emotional realism, cinematic composition.
```

## B-03 Image Prompt：教室角落特写

```text
Close-up of a Chinese teenage boy sitting in the corner of a classroom, tense jaw, guarded eyes, slight fatigue, silent pressure, shallow depth of field, grey-blue tones, cinematic 35mm film look, realistic and intimate.
```

## B-03 Video Prompt

```text
Very subtle movement: the boy breathes, blinks slowly, keeps his guarded expression while classroom noise continues around him, restrained emotional realism.
```

---

## B-04 Image Prompt：旧摩托接人

```text
After-school scene outside a Chinese school, a mother arrives on an old motorcycle while other families pick children up in cars, a teenage boy looks conflicted, realistic city-town setting, emotional contrast, cinematic natural light, photorealistic.
```

## B-05 Image Prompt：夜晚失眠

```text
Night interior, a Chinese teenage boy lying awake staring at the ceiling in silence, dim room, city light leaking through the window, lonely and heavy emotional atmosphere, cool-and-warm contrast, cinematic close framing, vintage film texture.
```

## B-05 Video Prompt

```text
The boy lies awake without speaking, slight breathing and eye movement, distant city light flickering through the window, silent pressure, restrained sadness, no dramatic acting.
```

## B-07 Image Prompt：冲突后沉默

```text
A Chinese teenage boy after an argument with his mother, lips pressed tight, eyes wet but holding back tears, domestic interior, silent and heavy atmosphere, realistic family drama, restrained emotion, photorealistic.
```
