# Sample A 模型投喂 Prompt 拆分包

> 用途：把 Sample A 的 prompt 拆成可直接复制到图像/视频模型里的逐镜头输入。

---

## 全局风格前缀

```text
Chinese realistic nostalgic drama, warm golden light, vintage 35mm film texture, gentle emotional storytelling, 1990s to early-2000s China, poetic realism, photorealistic, soft grain, natural light, restrained emotion
```

## 全局负面约束

```text
avoid anime style, avoid cyberpunk neon, avoid overly glossy commercial look, avoid western village, avoid fantasy architecture, avoid modern luxury interior, avoid exaggerated facial expression, avoid distorted hands, avoid text in image, avoid logo, avoid watermark
```

---

## A-01 Image Prompt：西山清晨

```text
Chinese realistic nostalgic drama, warm golden light, vintage 35mm film texture, gentle emotional storytelling, 1990s China, poetic realism. A cinematic wide aerial view of Xishan at sunrise, thin morning mist wrapping around the mountain, a quiet village waking at the foot of the mountain, warm golden-yellow light spilling over rooftops, soft grain, peaceful and emotional atmosphere, natural Chinese landscape, photorealistic.
```

## A-01 Video Prompt：西山清晨

```text
Slow smooth aerial push-in over Xishan at sunrise, mist gently drifting, morning light gradually warming the village below, calm cinematic movement, no abrupt camera motion, nostalgic 35mm film texture, poetic Chinese realism.
```

---

## A-02 Image Prompt：老屋外景

```text
Chinese realistic nostalgic drama, warm morning sunlight, vintage 35mm film texture. An old Chinese rural family house with weathered walls, sunflowers by the entrance, baby clothes hanging on a clothesline in a small courtyard, lived-in details, not a tourist homestay, humble and warm, early-2000s Chinese family memory, photorealistic.
```

## A-02 Video Prompt

```text
A gentle slow push-in toward the old rural house, baby clothes moving slightly in the breeze, warm morning light, quiet family atmosphere, no people, no exaggerated motion.
```

## A-04 Image Prompt：奶奶哄睡

```text
Interior night scene in an old Chinese family bedroom, an elderly Chinese grandmother gently patting a little boy to sleep on a simple bed, a vintage lamp casting soft shadows on the wall, intimate warm low light, tender family atmosphere, realistic Chinese grandmother, vintage 35mm film grain.
```

## A-04 Video Prompt

```text
Very subtle motion: grandmother gently pats the child's back, warm lamp shadow flickers softly on the wall, quiet breathing, slow emotional pacing, no dramatic camera movement.
```

## A-05 Image Prompt：父母背对背

```text
Cinematic medium shot inside an old Chinese home, father and mother sitting back-to-back in heavy silence, a small Chinese boy standing helpless at the doorway, tense family atmosphere, warm light mixed with dim interior shadows, restrained emotional realism, door-frame composition, vintage film grain.
```

## A-05 Video Prompt

```text
Static camera, heavy silence, slight dust in warm window light, the child frozen at the doorway, parents sitting back-to-back without speaking, subtle emotional tension, no melodramatic gestures.
```

---

## A-06 Image Prompt：父亲离开

```text
Long shot on a quiet Chinese village road, a father walking away carrying luggage, a small Chinese boy reaching out from the foreground, emotional distance emphasized by perspective, warm late-afternoon sunlight, poignant family separation, nostalgic cinematic realism, 35mm film texture.
```

## A-06 Video Prompt

```text
The father slowly walks away down the village road while the boy reaches out but cannot follow, gentle wind, warm light, static or very slow pull-back camera, restrained sadness.
```

## A-07-1 Image Prompt：公交乡村窗外

```text
Inside an old Chinese bus, a child sits by the window, rural village roads and fields passing outside, reflection on the glass, soft dusk light, nostalgic and bittersweet, early-2000s China, cinematic 35mm film texture.
```

## A-07-2 Image Prompt：公交城市窗外

```text
Inside an old Chinese bus, a child looking out the window as small-city streets pass by, apartment buildings and streetlights outside, face reflected faintly on the glass, lonely but quiet mood, nostalgic early-2000s China, realistic cinematic texture.
```

## A-07 Video Prompt

```text
Bus window montage, scenery changing from rural roads to small-city streets, the child's face reflected in the glass, soft dusk light, gentle vibration of the bus, melancholic but restrained.
```

## A-08 Image Prompt：奶奶送别与花生

```text
Emotional farewell at a Chinese village entrance, elderly Chinese grandmother pressing a small bag of roasted peanuts into a young boy's hand, a bus waiting nearby, warm dusk light, grandmother's eyes holding back tears, nostalgic family realism, cinematic close-up, photorealistic.
```

## A-08 Video Prompt

```text
Grandmother gently places the small bag of peanuts into the boy's hand, then stands at the village entrance waving as the bus leaves, the boy's face reflected in the bus window, warm heartbreaking dusk, slow soft dissolve, restrained emotion.
```

## A-09 Image Prompt：结尾定格

```text
A young Chinese boy sitting by an old bus window at dusk, holding a small bag of peanuts in his hand, his face reflected on the glass, outside the window the village road fades into warm evening light, nostalgic and emotional, ideal for title card, cinematic 35mm film texture.
```
