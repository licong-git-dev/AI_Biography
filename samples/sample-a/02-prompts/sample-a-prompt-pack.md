- 风格统一词：Chinese realistic nostalgic drama, warm golden light, vintage 35mm film texture, gentle emotional storytelling, 1990s to early-2000s China, poetic realism

**中文描述**：西山清晨，晨雾、日出、村庄苏醒。

**Video Prompt**
```text
Cinematic aerial drone shot, slow smooth push-in over Xishan at sunrise, thin morning mist, a quiet village waking at the foot of the mountain, warm golden-yellow light, nostalgic vintage 35mm film texture, poetic Chinese realism, soft grain, peaceful and emotional atmosphere, photorealistic, 8k.
```
